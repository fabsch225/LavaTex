# Uniqueness of limits

**Lemma** {#lem-unique}
A convergent sequence in a metric space has exactly one limit.
∎

**Proof**
If $x_n \to a$ and $x_n \to b$, then $d(a,b) \le d(a,x_n) + d(x_n,b) \to 0$.
∎

By [#lem-unique], we may write $\lim x_n$.
